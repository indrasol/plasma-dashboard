Typography

Lato - Body Font
The quick brown fox jumps
Used for body text throughout the application
Lato is a clean, modern sans-serif font that provides excellent readability for body text. Available in Regular (400) and Bold (700) weights. This is the primary font for all UI elements, paragraphs, and general content.
Roboto - Display Font
Analytics Dashboard
Used for page titles and major headings
Roboto Bold (700) is used exclusively for major headings and page titles, providing a strong visual hierarchy and professional appearance.
